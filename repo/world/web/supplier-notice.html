Swiftlink Supply Chain: Coating Batch Change Notice

In late June 2026, the supplier switched the supply batch of the waterproof coating used on tent fabric.

Some tents from the affected batch show degraded waterproofing under sustained rain, concentrated in Cloudrest 2 units shipped after that batch.

We recommend closer after-sales tracking of orders from this batch. This page is an internal supply-chain notice, for investigation reference only.
